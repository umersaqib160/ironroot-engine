## How to use this batch

**Two tracks of copy.** Organic posts (Instagram / Facebook / TikTok) can use the punchy education-and-fear angle — that's allowed and performs well on those feeds. **Meta paid ads cannot.** Each post now has a separate **META AD (policy-safe)** block written to survive ad review: positive framing, no "your pan is poisoning you" style second-person health attribution, no unverifiable claims. Run only the META AD blocks as paid ads.

**One consolidated campaign.** All META AD copy here is written for a single broad prospecting campaign (the €10/day all in one ad set), not three split campaigns. Rotate 3–4 of these as creatives, let Meta optimize, and add retargeting only once the pixel has data.

**Honesty guardrails (read before posting):**
- **No invented numbers.** You have ~27 real reviews. Do not say "thousands of customers" or "1,000+ reviews." Social-proof posts below quote real review *text* without claiming a fake scale. Fix the inflated counts on the store too before driving paid traffic.
- **Stainless isn't Teflon.** Don't promise effortless egg-release. Stainless is "naturally non-stick *when properly preheated*" — frame the technique, not a coating-like miracle, or you'll get returns.
- **Keep regulatory claims defensible.** The EPA has *moved to restrict / set limits on* certain PFAS — it has not "banned all PFAS in cookware." Wording below reflects that.
