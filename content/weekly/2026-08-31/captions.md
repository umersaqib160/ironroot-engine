**Scene:** a chef is scrubbing the inside of the pan hard with a metal scouring pad at the sink
**Mood:** dim light but cozy

**Claim:** there is no coating to ruin, so it can be scrubbed

> Source: Product description: "free of chemical coatings", "premium tri-ply stainless steel". The absence of a coating is the product's core fact, so the durability that follows from it is substantiated by construction.

**Caption MUST:** Lead with the CONTRAST, which is where the force is: do this to a non-stick pan and you have destroyed it. Bare stainless has nothing to scratch off, so a stuck-on mess is a scrub, not a replacement. This is the buy-it-for-life argument made concrete.

**Caption NEVER:** Do not present this as routine daily cleaning — for that use bd_easy_clean. This scene exists to show what the pan SURVIVES, not how it is normally washed.

**QC watch:** Check the finish still reads as clean satin stainless. If the model renders the pan as scratched, gouged or dulled, REJECT — the image would argue against the product instead of for it.
