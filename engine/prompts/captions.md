# Captions — per platform

One concept, four voices. Never paste the same caption to more than one platform.

---

## Instagram
Hook in the first line — it is all that shows before "more". Conversational, tight. One idea.
No link (bio only). 8–12 hashtags, mixed reach, on their own line.

## Facebook
Same idea, more room. Facebook audiences read: 3–5 sentences of actual argument is fine.
This is the one platform that gets the **store link**, once a week.

## Pinterest
Keyword-led, written for search rather than for a feed. Say what the thing *is* early
("PFAS-free stainless steel frying pan") because pins are found by query, not by follower.
Pins keep working for months — treat this as evergreen copy, not a post.

## TikTok
Caption is secondary to the on-screen hook, which must land in the **first 1–2 seconds**.
Short caption, 3–5 hashtags. One sound per clip, chosen in-app, never changed mid-clip.

---

## Applies to all four

- Voice per `../brand.md` §1: plain, direct, no hype.
- Never a claim the asset can't support.
- Never an invented number, review or milestone (`../brand.md` §5).
- Where the asset is AI-edited, the platform AI label goes **on** at posting.
- CTA is the offer, stated plainly: **$79 · PFAS-free · 30-day guarantee · free shipping.**
