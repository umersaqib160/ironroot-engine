### The one reservation: p2_mother_dinner (26 Oct)

It passes all four checks. Two things are worth Umer's eye anyway:

- The wall reads taller than the reference — not wok-deep, but the deepest pan
  in the batch.
- A sleeping baby sits in a bouncer beside a lit gas burner. Nothing is unsafe
  in the frame, but a brand is judged on what it appears to endorse.

Neither is a defect the gate should reject automatically. Both are a judgement
call, so it is Umer's.

## Rejected

ours. October also runs a card reading *"Your non-stick pan has PFAS. IronRoot
doesn't."* The photo argues against the card. The scene itself is fine — two
people cooking simply invites a second burner into frame — so it can come back
once a clean generation proves it.

round, and a shallow bowl-shaped body. An empty pan alone on a counter gives the
model nothing to anchor the shape to.

**p4_meal_prep** — Umer's own catch. A complete Instagram screenshot: carrier
name and clock, the wordmark, a username, like and comment icons, carousel dots,

**p2_chef_stirring**, **p2_plating_up** — rejected by Umer at the September
